LIQUID TV is an ultra-modern, open-source live TV and IPTV streaming client designed specifically for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV devices.

Features:
• Liquid Glass UI: Real-time frosted glass cards with specular highlights and smooth focus animations.
• ExoPlayer Media3 Streaming: Low-latency HLS (.m3u8), TS, and MPD live stream playback.
• 100% D-pad Navigation: Designed strictly for 10-foot TV remote control.
• Smart Channel Organization: Filter broadcast channels by Categories, Countries, and Languages.
• Favorites & History: Quick access to favorite channels and Continue Watching right on the Home screen.
• Built-in In-App Updater: Automatic GitHub release detection and one-click package installation.

Public Stream Attribution:
LIQUID TV uses community-curated playlists from iptv-org/iptv. Users can also configure any custom M3U/M3U8 playlist in Settings. LIQUID TV is an open-source media player and does not host or broadcast any media content.
